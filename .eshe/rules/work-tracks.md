# Choose the amount of process

Select at routing and reconsider when new evidence changes uncertainty or impact. Tracks control artifact size; they never remove host instructions, required proof, authority checks or risk overlays. UI research tiers remain independent.

| Track | Select when | Minimum record | Escalate when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Explore | Requested outcome is understanding, options or a feasibility decision | Question, known facts, bounded investigation, sources, conclusion and unresolved decision | Implementation is requested; re-lock the deliverable before changing product code |
| Quick | Intent and owner are clear; change is local, low impact and reversible; no unresolved affected decision | Compact task with acceptance, baseline, allowed change, next action and result | A contract, shared behavior, sensitive boundary, migration or material uncertainty appears |
| Standard | Feature/fix spans several steps or consumers inside established boundaries | Task, linked requirements, dependency plan, applicable decisions, proof and closure | New architecture, risky rollout, cross-repo coordination or uncertain recovery appears |
| Extended | Foundational choices, substantial uncertainty, cross-system work, sensitive transitions or costly/irreversible consequences | Standard plus affected decision/architecture/recovery records and explicit readiness review | Divide into coherent slices; do not force one huge execution packet |

Pick the strongest applicable condition. A one-line access-control change can be extended. Many safe copy changes can remain quick if their consumers and verification are understood. Record the reason, not just the label.

No track creates an approval ceremony. Proceed under existing authorization; ask only for a material missing decision or action authority. A clear small task may pass multiple lifecycle states in one turn. One compact record can contain several sections; separate files are optional until size, reuse or ownership makes them useful.

Use [planning](../flows/planning.md) for standard/extended execution and [exploration](../flows/explore.md) when the deliverable is a decision. For quick work apply the same readiness questions briefly within the task.
